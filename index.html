Privacy Policy for BlinkDrop

Effective date: 10 August 2026
Last updated: 10 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BlinkDrop ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. BlinkDrop is developed by Shreesha Innanje. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Summary (plain English)

  • BlinkDrop is a local Wi‑Fi file server. Your files are shared only across your own Wi‑Fi network, directly between your devices.
  • We do not upload your files to any cloud, and we do not collect, store, sell, or transmit your files or personal content to us or any third party.
  • The app works without an account or sign‑up.
  • Purchases are handled securely by Google Play Billing; we never see or store your payment details.

1. Information the app accesses on your device

To provide its core function, BlinkDrop requires access to files and media stored on your device. This is used only to list, preview, stream, download, and upload files that you choose to share while the server is running.

  • Photos & media (images, video, audio): read on the host device so they can be browsed and streamed by devices you connect.
  • Documents and all file types: because BlinkDrop is a file manager / file server, it can list and serve all of your files — including documents (PDF, DOC, TXT, ZIP, and others) — so you can browse and transfer them to your connected devices. On Android 11 and above this uses the All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) permission, which you grant manually in system settings.
  • Files and storage: read to list folders and files; write is used only to save files that another device uploads to your Downloads folder at your request.

This access happens entirely on your device. The contents of your files are transmitted only over your local Wi‑Fi network to devices that connect to your server. Your files are never sent to the developer or any external server.

2. Permissions we request and why

  • MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (All files access, Android 11+) — required so BlinkDrop can browse, preview, and serve all file types, including documents, as a file manager / local file server. You grant this manually in system settings, and it is used only to list and transfer files at your request. It is never used to read or upload your files to us or any third party.
  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — to browse, preview, and serve your media/files on devices or Android versions where All files access is not used.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 9 and below) — to save files uploaded from a connected device.
  • INTERNET, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to run the local web server on your Wi‑Fi and show the connection address. The app does not require or use an internet/cloud connection to transfer your files.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC, POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to keep the server running reliably and show its status in a notification.

3. How your files are transmitted

When you start the server, BlinkDrop hosts your selected content over your local Wi‑Fi network using a standard HTTP connection on your device's local IP address (default port 8080). Any device on the same Wi‑Fi network that opens that address (for example by scanning the QR code) can browse and transfer the shared files while the server is running.

Security note: The local connection is unencrypted (HTTP) and is not password‑protected. Only start the server on Wi‑Fi networks you trust, and stop the server when you are finished. We recommend not running the server on public or untrusted networks.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

BlinkDrop offers a paid unlock (in‑app purchase) after a limited free usage allowance. All payments are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your name, card number, or payment information. Google may share limited purchase information (such as an anonymized purchase token and purchase status) with us so we can verify and restore your purchase. Google's handling of your payment data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

The app may store a local record on your device (for example, how many times the server has been started and whether the unlock has been purchased) solely to enforce the free‑usage limit. This information stays on your device.

5. Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your files, photos, videos, audio, or documents.
  • We do not require an account, email, name, or phone number.
  • The app contains no third‑party advertising and no third‑party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share any personal information.

6. Data retention

Because we do not collect your content, there is nothing for us to retain. Any local settings (such as the usage counter or purchase status) remain on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

7. Children's privacy

BlinkDrop is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Since the app does not collect personal data on our servers, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, or delete. You can revoke the app's permissions at any time in your device settings, and you can remove all local app data by uninstalling the app.
